# Privacy Note

**Plain English. Short sentences. Easy words.**

---

## What we collect

We collect only what you give us:

- Your name (or what you want us to call you)
- Your phone number or email address
- Your document type (we do not store a copy of your document)
- Your support and accommodation needs

---

## What we do NOT collect

- We do NOT collect medical information.
- We do NOT collect diagnoses or disability certificates.
- We do NOT take photos of your ID.
- We do NOT track your location.
- We do NOT share your data with advertisers.

---

## Your support request is private

When you ask for support or accommodations:

- Your request is saved only on your device first.
- It is never shared with employers without your clear permission.
- You choose what employers can see.

---

## Voice input

When you use voice typing:

- Your spoken words go to your browser's speech service.
- They are used only to fill in your answer.
- They are not saved or used for advertising.
- We ask your permission before using voice typing for the first time.

---

## Your control

- You can delete your data at any time.
- You can save your progress and come back later.
- You can ask a support advisor to help you delete your account.

---

## Questions?

If you have questions about your data, contact us through the Help button on any screen.
