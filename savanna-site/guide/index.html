Accessible onboarding on Savanna

Demo guide

ALX's Savanna learner pages, with three forms built and published in Allora Accessibility Bridge and placed on the pages with the one embed tag the Bridge generates: sign in and sign up, learner registration, and a job application. Every form can be used by keyboard, by voice and with a screen reader, in English or Amharic.

Where to begin

Start at the sign-in page, then follow the steps below. Each step opens a real page of the demo.

Walk through it in four steps

  1. 1

    Sign in, or create an account

    The sign-in page shows ALX's published Sign up / Sign in form: an email address, then a password, one at a time. Its own New here? Create an account link switches it to sign-up (name, email, password), and the page's heading follows. The Sign in | Create account switch above the form does the same, and Create an account opens it in sign-up straight away. The Create an account button at the top of this guide opens the same sign-up form here, in a pop-up. Every form also has a Full view button that opens it larger, over a blurred page.

    This demo has no accounts to check or create. When you continue, the page removes the password in your browser straight away, sends nothing, and brings you here.

  2. 2

    Look around the learner portal

    Open the self-paced learner homepage. The sidebar has a Get hired group, and a Ready for work? card sits under the dashboard's cards. Both lead to the two forms.

  3. 3

    Register as a learner

    Open Register. The form appears in the portal's own card and asks one question per screen. Things to try:

    • Use only the keyboard: Tab to move, Enter to answer and go on.
    • Answer by speaking, and Read aloud on any question.
    • Settings at the top: text size, contrast, simple words and Amharic.
    • Choices show in full lists, without opening anything.
    • Reload halfway through: you carry on where you were (private answers are not kept).
    • Support needs are asked in a private section of their own.
    • After sending, open What this page received to see the answers as JSON.
  4. 4

    Apply for a job

    Open Apply for a job. It adds a CV upload (try a file of the wrong type, or one over the size limit), private interview accommodations, and How can we identify you?: a document with its number, or I have no document, refer me, which offers to text a referral partner.

    No text-message provider is set up in this demo, so a referral says honestly that nothing was sent.

How the forms work inside Savanna

One tag per form

Each page holds a <script> and an <onboarding-form> element, copied from the form's Embed tag page in the Bridge. View any page's source to see it. Nothing else of the form lives in Savanna.

A fixed, published version

A tag names one published version. Changing a form in the Bridge shows here only after it is published again and the new tag is pasted in.

Only allowed websites

The Bridge serves these forms only to the websites ALX has listed, so a copied tag does not work anywhere else.

Answers go to the page, not the Bridge

When a learner sends a form, it hands every answer to this page as JSON, for ALX's own code to save or process. Allora Accessibility Bridge never receives or keeps them. ALX can also have them posted straight to its own server.

Before you present it

The demo runs on this computer. For every step to work:

  • The Bridge's API is running (npm run api), and the forms are published.
  • Savanna is opened at exactly http://localhost:8080. Another address, such as 127.0.0.1:8080, counts as a different website and is refused.
  • Optional: to show answers also arriving on a server of ALX's own, run the example receiver (npm run delivery:receiver in the Bridge) and save its address under Your organisation → Where submitted forms go.
